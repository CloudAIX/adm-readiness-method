# Scan data — deliberately not published

This directory holds identifiable results: the domains scanned, per-practice
findings, judged verdicts and retrieved policy text. **None of it is committed.**

Publishing a named list of healthcare practices against a compliance gap
would be publishing their exposure, not our work. It would also be wrong on
the facts: the obligation does not commence until 10 December 2026, so no
practice scanned is in breach of anything.

What is published instead:
- the method (`scanner/`, `agents/`, `tests/`)
- aggregate findings (`docs/FINDINGS.md`)
- the root cause, which sits with a public template, not with any practice

To reproduce, supply your own `data/domains.txt`, one domain per line.
